Towards Automated Music Arrangement

This project uses a Transformer-based model to sort notes from unlabeled MIDI files into instrument channels by predicting the best part assignment for each note. The model focuses on features such as pitch, velocity, timing, and note duration; by comparing the relationships between all notes to infer their grouping. This task is a step towards automating initial music arrangement, such as transforming a single instrument performance or noisy MIDI outputs (from MP3-to-MIDI conversion) into multi-instrument arrangements by identifying the voicings. The model was trained using a subset of the Lakh MIDI Dataset, which provides MIDI files with pre-labeled instrumental parts. During training, channel labels were anonymized, then evaluated based on the model's ability to cluster notes into coherent instrumental groupings correctly. The current model is 61% accurate in sorting notes across the testing MIDI files, leaving plenty of room for future improvement.
